The call kit

Six questions. Any good reglazer answers all six easily.

How many years on the warranty, in writing?Under 5 for a tub, keep calling.
Is it a spray coating or a poured slipcover?You want sprayed. Aliphatic acrylic urethane is the good answer.
Tub AND wall tile in one visit, one cure?One mobilization saves money and mess.
How long before light use, and before normal use?Real answer: 24-72 hours light, about 30 days babied.
Do you mask and ventilate, or do I prep?Pros bring their own containment. Your fan helps anyway.
What voids the warranty?They should say suction mats and abrasives without being asked.
Red flags: cash only, no written warranty, "same day use," a price under $300 for a tub. Hang up nice.

After the pro leaves

The fridge card

New glaze. House rules.

  • No water for 72 hours. Nothing. Not once.
  • No suction mats. Ever. Number one cause of peeling.
  • Non-abrasive cleaner only. No scrub powder, no magic erasers.
  • Baby it 30 days. Wipe, don't scour. Nothing stored on the tub edge.
  • See a chip? Call the pro. Warranty covers it. Picking at it doesn't.
