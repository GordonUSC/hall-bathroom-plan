Before choosing a reglazer

  • What exactly is included: tub, wet walls, other tile?
  • How are repairs, preparation and ventilation handled?
  • Which coating system, written warranty and exclusions?
  • When can the room be entered, touched and used?
  • What cleaners, mats and accessories are allowed?
  • Who protects the house, removes waste and handles callbacks?

Get the installer’s written cure and aftercare instructions. A universal countdown cannot replace them.
